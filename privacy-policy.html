Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Feldman Flashlight, published by Feldman. It explains what the app accesses, what it stores, and the choices available to you. Effective August 24, 2026.

Data collection and sharing

Feldman Flashlight does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. The app does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts, or behavioral tracking.

Because the app does not collect personal data, Feldman does not retain personal data on a server and has no personal server-side profile to delete.

On-device access

Camera flash

The app uses Android's camera system service only to control the device's flash hardware and read whether that hardware is available. It does not capture, store, or transmit photos, video, or audio.

Ambient light sensor

If your device provides an ambient light sensor, its current reading is processed temporarily to show luminance in the interface. Readings are not saved or transmitted.

Quick Settings

The app can ask Android to add its flashlight tile. Android handles that request, and the tile only controls the flashlight and opens the app's tile settings.

Local settings, retention, and deletion

Preferences such as theme, brightness, light source, screen-light color, automatic shutoff, and tile behavior are stored locally in Android app storage.

They remain until you change them, clear Feldman Flashlight's storage, or uninstall the app. Android may include these preferences in device backup and restore if backup is enabled in your system settings. Feldman cannot access those backups.

Security and third-party code

The app relies on Android platform services and open-source interface libraries. It does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Local settings are protected by Android's app sandbox and are not intentionally exposed to other apps.

Children's privacy

Feldman Flashlight is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those practices take effect, and the effective date will be revised.

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker. The app source is available in the Feldman Flashlight repository.
